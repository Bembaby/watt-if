# Watt If visual direction

The current revision follows the rendered Hermes Agent reference closely in composition: thin condensed title-case display type, symmetric navigation around a stacked wordmark, a granular midnight surface, oversized sculptural imagery, subdued charcoal actions and a vivid cobalt working panel. Watt If has its own identity, words, artwork and household workflow.

The subject is an evening that can be rearranged. A real marble relief, Thorvaldsen's Night, sits inside original clock rings. Its photographed material replaces the previous illustrative engraving. The original photo is distributed unchanged; SVG clipping and CSS grayscale create the presentation. Source and CC0 reuse license are recorded in ASSET_CREDITS.md. No Hermes illustration or logo is imported.

Tokens: midnight #0b0d21; cobalt #3934fa; ivory #eeece3; paper #e6e4da; paper ink #222334; muted green #cbd5b7. Barlow Condensed Light carries the large headings and wordmark; SemiBold carries compact navigation and labels; Manrope carries working content. Fonts are self-hosted with their OFL licenses.

Layout: a quiet left-aligned introduction beside the large marble clock; a cobalt request surface beside an inset paper timetable. On a phone, the sculpture follows the introduction and the planner stacks into two usable surfaces. Different texture scales, inset shading, physical edges and original SVG grain create layers rather than flat cards. The plan stays legible and the artwork leaves the working area uncluttered.

The planner preserves its actual state, pending-request recovery, inspected constraints and revision-bound approval. Empty, unavailable, pending, conflict, review, approved and exported states remain distinct. The technical trace stays collapsed. No invented progress, integrations, adoption or measured savings are displayed. Motion is limited to user feedback and disabled for reduced-motion preferences.
